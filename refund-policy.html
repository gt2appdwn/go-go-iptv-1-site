GO GO IPTV Refund Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Our Refund Promise

We offer a 7-day money-back guarantee for new orders. Read below for how refunds work.

1. Who Qualifies

You can request a refund within 7 days of your order. Please use the email you ordered with, include your order ID, and try our support team for help first.

2. When No Refund Is Given

We do not refund after 7 days, or if your account is closed for breaking our terms such as sharing logins or re-streaming. Renewals after a full period and problems from your own internet are also not refunded.

3. How to Ask

Send an email to refunds@iptvgogo.org with your order ID. We will review your order date and then turn off your login details if you qualify.

4. Refund Payment Methods

Refunds go back to your original payment method (card, Apple Pay, PayPal, or crypto). How long it takes depends on your bank or wallet.

5. Digital Delivery

Your login details come by email within 30 minutes of payment confirmation. Please check your spam folder if you do not see them. Nothing is shipped.
